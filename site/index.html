MORE THAN A CAR CLUB

A place for every
kind of enthusiast.

Founded in 1997 by a local group of car enthusiasts, the Weevil City Cruisers bring people together over a love of cars and trucks, both old and new.

You don’t need a classic car to join the fun. Bring a new car, an old truck, or simply your enthusiasm. Everyone with an interest in cars and trucks is welcome.

Community has always been part of the club’s story, with a tradition of supporting local charities and gathering for car shows, cruise-ins, and club activities.
